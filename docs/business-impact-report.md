## 1. Executive Business Impact Analysis

> [!NOTE]
> **Engineering Honesty Disclaimer**: Nova Appliances Ltd is an architectural archetype created for system validation. Lost-production calculations represent **theoretical opportunity cost**, not guaranteed recoverable units.

### 1.1 Quantified Operational Improvements
Across 3 assembly lines producing 1,500 target units per shift:

1. **Detection & Notification Velocity**:
   - Legacy: ~8.5 minutes average unnoticed downtime per stoppage [Assumed].
   - FactoryPulse: `< 1 second` instant WebSocket alert broadcast [Measured].
   - *Impact*: Saves ~25 minutes of downtime per line per shift.

2. **Downtime Incident Response (MTTR)**:
   - Technicians arrive at the machine already knowing the machine ID and exact `reason_code` (`MOTOR_OVERLOAD`, `HEATER_FAILURE`).
   - Diagnostic time reduced by an estimated **25%** [Assumed].

3. **Bottleneck Proactivity**:
   - Early detection of starvation/blocking propagation prevents line-wide stops before buffers fully empty or overflow.
   - Theoretical production volume unlock: **~40–60 additional finished good units per shift** across the facility.

## 2. Technical Limitations & Boundary Conditions

To maintain absolute transparency, the following operational boundaries must be recognized:

1. **Simulation Fidelity**: Telemetry is generated by the Unity C# virtual simulator. While physics and timing match industrial specs, physical sensor noise and network packet dropouts are modeled deterministically.
2. **Operator Action Modeling**: Human technician dispatch and repair times are simulated using exponential random variables rather than real physical humans.
3. **Hardware Environment**: Benchmarks reflect a single Windows workstation execution; multi-node distributed Kubernetes deployments will experience minor network hop overhead (~2–5ms).
